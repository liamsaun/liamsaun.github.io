About Me

I'm William 'Liam' Saunders, a Chemical Engineering student at the University of Arizona. I'm passionate about using engineering principles to solve complex problems and make technology more accessible to everyone.

My interest in chemical engineering stems from a desire to create practical solutions that improve how materials, processes, and products work. Through my work on low-cost prosthetics, I've learned the importance of combining technical innovation with real-world accessibility and user-centered design.

Outside of engineering, I'm a competitive Pokemon Trading Card Game player, where my passion for mathematics and statistics really shines. Building optimal decks and making strategic decisions under pressure has sharpened my analytical thinking and problem-solving skills—abilities that translate directly into my engineering work.

As I continue my studies, I'm excited to explore process design, materials science, energy systems, and sustainable manufacturing, while collaborating with others who share my passion for thoughtful and affordable engineering.
